Quick Tips

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take for a report to be resolved?
It depends on the severity and location, but most reports are reviewed within a few days.
Can I edit a report after submitting it?
Not currently — but you can delete it from My Reports and submit a new one if needed.
Why did I receive a warning notification?
Admins send warnings if a report appears inappropriate, spam, or inaccurate. Please review the message for details.
